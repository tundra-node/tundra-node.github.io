Monorepo for two I2P tools.

Abyssal is a public I2P search engine with a web crawler, Wayback archive, full-text search, site directory, and link graph. Prism is a private i2pd router dashboard with a browser proxy and addressbook manager.

Self-hosted I2P search plus browsing, all over the I2P network.
