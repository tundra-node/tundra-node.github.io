Happy New Year! Here's what I actually use every day. Most of this stuff is free and privacy-focused.

## Essential Sites

[FMHY](https://fmhy.net) - Giant wiki of free stuff. I check this before googling anything.

[DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com) - Search engine that doesn't track you. The email masking feature is great for avoiding spam.

[LibreWolf](https://librewolf.net) - Firefox without the telemetry. Has uBlock built in.

## YouTube Extensions

[uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com) - Blocks everything. Required.

[SponsorBlock](https://sponsor.ajay.app) - Skips the "this video is sponsored by" stuff automatically.

[Return YouTube Dislikes](https://returnyoutubedislike.com) - Brings back the dislike count.

[Unhook](https://unhook.app) - Hides recommendations so you only watch what you came for.

[DeArrow](https://dearrow.ajay.app) - Replaces clickbait thumbnails with actual screenshots.

## Browser Extensions

[Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com) - Password manager. You can self-host it if you want.

[Dark Reader](https://darkreader.org) - Forces dark mode on everything.

[Sink It for Reddit](https://gosinkit.com/) - Removes app nags and login popups. Also blocks ads and adds color-coded comments.

## Library Services

[Libby](https://libbyapp.com) - Free ebooks and audiobooks through your library card.

[Hoopla](https://www.hoopladigital.com) - Same deal but no waitlists. You get a monthly limit though.

[Kanopy](https://www.kanopy.com) - Free movies through your library. Lots of weird artsy stuff.

## Homelab

[Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org) - Media server. Like Plex but actually free.

[Immich](https://immich.app) - Google Photos replacement you run yourself.

[Navidrome](https://www.navidrome.org) - Stream your music from anywhere.

[Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io) - Smart home stuff without the cloud.

[Proxmox](https://www.proxmox.com) - Run a bunch of VMs on one machine.

[*arr apps](https://wiki.servarr.com) - Sonarr, Radarr, etc. Automates media downloading and organizing.

## Desktop Apps

[Karabiner Elements](https://karabiner-elements.pqrs.org) - Remap any key to anything on Mac.

[Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) - Note-taking with markdown files and internal links.

[Crossover](https://www.codeweavers.com/crossover) - Run Windows apps without a VM.

[Tuta Mail](https://tuta.com) - Encrypted email.

[Oversight](https://objective-see.org/products/oversight.html) - Alerts you when your webcam or mic turns on.

[LuLu](https://objective-see.org/products/lulu.html) - Firewall that shows what's calling home.

[Alacritty](https://alacritty.org) - Fast terminal. GPU accelerated.

[Keka](https://www.keka.io) - Unzip anything.

[KnockKnock](https://objective-see.org/products/knockknock.html) - See what launches on startup.

[LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org) - Free office suite. Opens Word/Excel files.

[Grayjay](https://grayjay.app) - Watch YouTube and Twitch in one app.

[IINA](https://iina.io) - Video player that just works.

[Cyberduck](https://cyberduck.io) - FTP client.

[Cloudflare WARP](https://1.1.1.1) - Free VPN that's actually fast.

[BoringNotch](https://github.com/iSapozhnik/BoringNotch) - Makes the notch useful.

[Raycast](https://www.raycast.com) - Better Spotlight.

[VSCodium](https://vscodium.com) - VS Code without Microsoft tracking.
